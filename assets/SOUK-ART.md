# Website courtyard illustrations

These PNG assets were generated for the SOUK-SCAN website. They are original
illustrations, rather than assets extracted from Farm Frenzy.

- `souk-medina-square.png`: paved Moroccan medina backdrop.
- `souk-vendors-rubber-hose.png`: vendor kiosks in a 2 × 2 sprite sheet.
- `souk-food-rubber-hose.png`: food illustrations in a 4 × 4 sprite sheet.

The originals are retained in the workspace's `generated_images` directory.
CSS selects each sprite. Right-side stalls face inward through a horizontal
flip of the illustration; native category text stays upright.

The logo in `brand/` is the existing SOUK-SCAN logo. Older product artwork and
its attribution in `products/ATTRIBUTION.md` are separate from these assets.
