App terms version: 12 October 2026 · Website updated: 20 October 2026

SOUK-SCAN is an informational tool that makes food prices in Morocco easier to see and compare. It does not sell products, set or control market prices, guarantee the prices any seller offers, or act as a marketplace or a regulator. It is not a government service.

What the prices are

Prices shown as community prices are reports submitted by users. They can be inaccurate, incomplete, out of date or unrepresentative, and they do not necessarily reflect every shop or market. They are shown only when enough recent, valid reports exist.

Reference prices

Where available, wholesale reference prices come from the published bulletins of Casa Prestations (casaprestations.ma). Each reference shows its source and the date of the bulletin it comes from. These are wholesale prices, which are not necessarily retail prices. When a product is missing from the latest bulletin, the app may show its most recent earlier reference, clearly labelled with its date. SOUK-SCAN is not endorsed by, affiliated with or partnered with Casa Prestations.

Using SOUK-SCAN responsibly

When you submit a price, please report what you genuinely saw or paid. Do not submit false or deliberately misleading prices, try to manipulate averages, abuse or overload the service, send spam, or submit unlawful or harmful content.

Moderation

We may review, flag, reject, hide or remove submissions that appear unreliable or abusive, and may restrict or close accounts that misuse the service. An unusual price is not automatically treated as a wrong one.

Limits of the information

Prices vary by location, market, product quality, unit and date. Please check current prices locally before relying on any figure for an important decision.
