Delete your SOUK-SCAN account

Last updated: 20 October 2026

You can permanently delete your SOUK-SCAN account and its associated data in the app or request deletion by email. This page applies to com.soukscan.app.

Delete in the app

Open SOUK-SCAN and sign in. Go to Profile → Delete account, review the information and confirm deletion.

Successful in-app deletion takes effect when the server confirms it. The app then signs you out. If deletion fails, you see an error and can retry. Deletion is permanent. Administrator accounts require assistance from the service maintainers.

Request deletion without opening the app

Email sanamdev@soukscan.online from the address registered with your account. Use the subject SOUK-SCAN account deletion request. If you used Google sign-in, write from that Google account’s address. Do not send your password.

We may ask you to confirm that you control the registered email address. We complete valid email deletion requests within 30 calendar days of receipt and reply when deletion is complete.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes your active account and sign-in identity, submitted price reports, feedback and linked terms-acceptance records. Removed reports no longer contribute to estimates computed from the remaining reports. Individual reports and account identities were not displayed publicly.

What may remain

Service-provider technical logs and backups, if retained, follow the providers’ retention policies rather than immediate active-account deletion. Preferences saved only on your device may remain until you clear the app’s storage or browser data.

Privacy questions

For questions about deletion or another data request, email sanamdev@soukscan.online. Read the privacy policy for the full description of data handling.
