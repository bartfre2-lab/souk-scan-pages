# Understand the prices

Community estimates and wholesale references answer different questions. Here is how to read them.

Estimates use reports for the selected region, when enough valid information is available. They may vary by quality, place and contributions.

Casa Prestations bulletins refer to Casablanca’s wholesale market. They are dated references, not guaranteed retail prices in your region.

Look for DH/kg or DH per egg, depending on the product. Read the source and date alongside the amount.

An honest empty state is more useful than a made-up price. Treat the information as context for your shopping.
