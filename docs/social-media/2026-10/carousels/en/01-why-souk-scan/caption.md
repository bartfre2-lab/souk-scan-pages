# Why SOUK-SCAN?

What is SOUK-SCAN for? Discover the idea in five slides.

The app helps people in Morocco explore food-price information. It does not sell food or set market prices.

Select one of Morocco’s 12 regions. Community estimates relate to the region you choose.

Fruit, vegetables, meat, poultry and eggs. Open a product to understand the available price information.

Explore the app, understand its sources and share a price you genuinely observed. Start at soukscan.online.
