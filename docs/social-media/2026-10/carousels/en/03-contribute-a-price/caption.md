# How to contribute

Saw or paid a food price? Share that real observation in SOUK-SCAN. Here is how.

Sign in, open the product page and confirm the region where you observed the price.

Use the price you actually saw or paid today. Check whether the product is priced per kilogram or per egg, then submit.

Need to correct it? Edit your report on the same day. The app keeps one report per product per day for your account.

Keep your regular products in favorites and contribute real observations. Arabic, French or English: discover SOUK-SCAN at soukscan.online.
