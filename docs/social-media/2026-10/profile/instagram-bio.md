# Instagram profile — SOUK-SCAN

## Profile picture

`instagram-profile-picture.png` (1080 × 1080): the app's own symbol (scanner
frame, location pin, market stall and produce) in white on the brand forest
green, from `SOUK-SCAN/resources/icon-only.png`. Instagram crops profile photos
to a circle; the symbol sits well inside the safe area, so nothing is cut off.
The rounded-square logo with the "SOUK-SCAN" wordmark is not used because its
transparent corners and text would be cropped in a circle.
