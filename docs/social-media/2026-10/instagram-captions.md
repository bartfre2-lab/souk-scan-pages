# Instagram captions — October 2026 carousels

One caption per carousel, per language (9 total). Post the five numbered slides
of a carousel together, one language per post, with the matching caption.

Notes:

- Instagram captions do not make links clickable: each caption points to the
  "link in bio" / "lien en bio" / "الرابط في البايو". Put `https://soukscan.online/`
  in the bio. Replace the bio line if you prefer to paste the URL.
- Wording follows the campaign's accuracy rules: community estimates are not
  wholesale prices, no guaranteed prices or savings, one report per product per
  day, no partnership claim, no Google Play claim.
- The first line is the hook (what shows before "more"). Each caption is well
  under Instagram's 2,200-character limit.

## Carousel 2 — Understand the prices (`02-understand-the-prices`)

### English

```text
Two numbers, two different questions. Here's how to read the prices in SOUK-SCAN. 🔍

📌 Community estimates: based on reports from the region you selected, when enough valid information is available. They can vary with quality, place and contributions.

📌 Wholesale references: dated Casa Prestations bulletins for Casablanca's wholesale market. They are references, not guaranteed retail prices in your region.

Before you compare:
✅ check the unit (DH/kg or DH per egg)
✅ read the source and the date next to the amount

And when there isn't enough data? The app says so. An honest empty state beats a made-up price.

Use the information as context for your shopping.

👉 Link in bio: soukscan.online
Save this post for the next time you compare prices. 🔖

#SOUKSCAN #Maroc #Morocco #PrixAlimentaires #FoodPrices #Transparence #Souk
```

```text
1️⃣ Sign in and open the product page
2️⃣ Confirm the region where you saw the price
3️⃣ Enter the price you actually saw or paid today, and check the unit (per kg or per egg)
4️⃣ Submit it

Only share what you genuinely observed, that's what keeps the estimates useful for everyone.

SOUK-SCAN works in Arabic, French and English.
```
