APP NAME (9/30 characters)
SOUK-SCAN

SHORT DESCRIPTION
See fair, clear food prices in Morocco, shared by real people.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Is this price fair? Now you can check before you pay.

SOUK-SCAN helps you see clear, honest prices for everyday food in Morocco: fruit, vegetables, meat, poultry and eggs. Prices can change from one market to the next, and it is hard to know what is fair. SOUK-SCAN exists for one simple reason: to make prices easy to see, so nobody has to guess.

WHERE THE PRICES COME FROM
• Real people. Users like you share what they saw or paid. We combine many reports into one price range for your region.
• Published wholesale prices. Dated wholesale references from Casa Prestations, shown next to the community prices so you can compare.
When there are not enough reports, the app tells you. It never invents a price.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• The souk: see today's price range for each product in your region.
• Al-Qoffa: build a weekly basket of everyday products and see an indicative range for what it may cost.
• Calculator: add up your shopping, shop by shop. Tap a price and a quantity, and the total follows.
• Info: read news from the SOUK-SCAN team.
• Profile and My journal: see the prices you added, day by day. Only you can see them.
• Share a price you really saw or paid: one price per product per day, and you can correct it the same day.
• A short guide shows you around the first time you open the app.
• Use English, French or Arabic. Arabic is a true right-to-left app. Light and dark modes are available.

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS
• Your exact location is not sent to SOUK-SCAN. Only your region is used.
• Other users never see your name, your individual prices or your shopping list.
• You can delete your account at any time in the app.
