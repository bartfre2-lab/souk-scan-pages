SOUK-SCAN

Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

SOUK-SCAN is a community food-price information app for Morocco (Android package com.soukscan.app). This policy explains how the app and this website handle information. For privacy questions or requests, contact sanamdev@soukscan.online.

Information the app processes

Account information includes your email address, account identifier and authentication records. Google sign-in may also provide account profile information to the authentication provider. We record acceptance of the app’s terms.

Price reports contain the product, price, region, date and the account identifier needed to manage your submissions. Feedback contains the category, message and account identifier you send.

Location and region

Automatic region detection is optional. With permission, the app reads a location on your device and matches it to one of Morocco’s 12 regions. The app does not transmit, persist or log the raw GPS coordinates. You can select a region manually instead.

The selected or detected region is saved on your device and sent to the backend when requesting regional prices or submitting a report. Reports retain that region alongside the account identifier. Keeping raw coordinates local does not mean that no region information leaves the device.

What other users can see

Public community prices are aggregated estimates. Your email, account identity and individual reports are not displayed publicly. Feedback is private to the service’s maintainers. Dated wholesale reference figures, where available, are a separate source.

Service providers and technical information

Supabase provides the app’s database and authentication services. Google handles Google sign-in when you choose it. GitHub Pages hosts this website. These services receive technical connection information, such as IP addresses and request information, needed to operate and secure their services.

We do not sell personal data, run ads, or include advertising or third-party analytics tools in the reviewed app or website. Processing by a hosting or authentication provider is different from publishing information to other users.

Storage on your device

The app stores preferences such as language, appearance, selected region and favourites on your device, along with the session information needed to keep you signed in. This website stores language, appearance and selected-region preferences on your device. It sends the selected region to Supabase when requesting community estimates; it does not ask for GPS access or sign you in. You can remove local preferences through your device or browser storage settings.

Retention and deletion

Account information, linked reports, feedback and terms-acceptance records are kept while needed to operate your account and the service, until account deletion or earlier removal. A limited display window for prices is not a promise that underlying reports are automatically erased after that window.

Confirmed in-app deletion removes the active account and its linked reports and feedback. Email deletion requests are completed within 30 calendar days after a valid request is received. See account deletion for the steps and scope.

Operational logs or backup copies retained by service providers follow their retention policies. Active-account deletion does not promise immediate removal of every provider log or backup. Local device preferences can remain until you clear the app or browser storage.

Security and your choices

The app communicates with its backend over HTTPS and uses authenticated access controls for private account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The app never receives your Google password.

You can deny location permission, choose a region manually, change language or appearance, sign out, or request account deletion. For access, correction or other privacy questions, contact sanamdev@soukscan.online.

Changes and contact

We update this page when the service’s data practices change. The date above identifies this website policy revision. Contact the SOUK-SCAN maintainers at sanamdev@soukscan.online.
